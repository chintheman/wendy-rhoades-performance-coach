# How the Setup Works — For Regular People

No code, no jargon. Here's what it actually takes to have this coach in your pocket, explained like you've never set up an AI before.

> **Before you set anything up:** this is a performance coach, not a mental-health treatment tool, and it's not affiliated with the TV show it's inspired by. **If you or someone you know is in crisis right now:** in the US call or text **988**; in Singapore call **SOS at 1767** or **995**; elsewhere, find a line at https://findahelpline.com. (More on this below, under "One important thing to change first.")

---

## The big idea in one paragraph

An AI chatbot (like ChatGPT or Claude) is a blank actor. It'll play any role you describe. This project is a **very detailed script** for one specific role: a sharp, honest performance coach. You hand the AI the script once, and from then on it stays in character. That's the whole trick. There's no app to install and nothing to buy beyond an AI account you may already have.

---

## What you need

1. **An AI account.** A free or paid ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini account. Any of them works.
2. **The script.** Three text files from this project (they're the coach's personality, techniques, and rules). You copy-paste them.
3. **Ten minutes.**

That's it.

---

## The setup, step by step

Think of it like training a new assistant on their first day — except you only have to do it once.

**Step 1 — Get the script.**
In this project, open the folder called `persona`. Inside are three files. Copy the text from all three, one after another, into a single note or document. This combined text is your coach's "job description" — who they are, how they work, and the lines they never cross.

**Step 2 — Give it to your AI.**
- **In ChatGPT:** use the "Create a GPT" feature. There's a big box labeled "Instructions." Paste your combined text there. Give it a name (call it "Coach" or whatever you like). Save.
- **In Claude:** make a "Project," and paste the text into the project's instructions box. Save.
- **In Gemini:** make a "Gem," paste the text into its instructions. Save.

**Step 3 — Talk to it.**
Open a chat with your new coach and bring a real situation. Not "hello" — an actual thing on your mind. For example:
- _"I have a big meeting in an hour and I've gone blank."_
- _"I keep saying yes to projects I end up hating. I did it again yesterday."_
- _"I've been working nonstop and getting nowhere and I don't understand why."_

It'll respond like a coach, not a search engine. It'll ask you pointed questions. It might push back. That's the point.

## One important thing to change first

Buried in the rules file is a list of emergency phone numbers, for the rare case where a conversation turns into something serious that needs a real professional. The defaults are US and Singapore numbers. **If you live somewhere else, swap in your country's crisis line before you use it** (or before you share it with anyone). Two minutes, and it matters.

## What using it actually feels like

You: _"I bombed a presentation today and I keep doing this."_

Most chatbots: _"I'm sorry to hear that! Here are 10 tips for better presentations..."_

This coach: _"That's two things. First — you bombed a presentation today, and that stings. What happened in the room? ...Okay. Now the other part: you said you 'keep doing this.' That's not about today's slides. That's a pattern. What are you afraid would happen if you didn't choke?"_

It separates the fresh wound from the old pattern. It doesn't dump advice on you. It gets you to the real thing.

## Common questions

**Do I need to pay for anything?**
No, beyond an AI account (many are free). This project is free and open-source.

**Will it remember me between conversations?**
Depends on your AI. ChatGPT and Claude have memory features that help. For the coach to notice patterns *across* weeks ("that's the third time you've said that"), it needs to be able to see your past chats — the more advanced setups in this project handle that automatically, but for the simple version, keeping one long-running chat works best.

**Is it safe? Is my stuff private?**
Your conversations are as private as your AI account is — read your provider's privacy policy. The coach itself is built to protect you: it won't tell you to do something reckless, won't pretend to be a therapist, and will steer you to real help if something serious comes up.

**Is this a replacement for therapy?**
No. It's a performance coach — for decisions, ruts, big moments, and burnout. It's specifically built to recognize when something is beyond coaching and to send you to a real professional. If you're struggling with your mental health, please talk to a doctor or therapist.

**Can I change how blunt it is?**
Yes. You can tell it to dial up (very blunt) or down (warm and gentle). Just say so. The one exception: if you're clearly in a bad place, it'll be gentle regardless of what you asked for — and tell you why.
