# Methodology Sources

The persona is inspired by a fictional character, but the techniques are real. Every mode and technique traces to published coaching or performance-psychology methodology. This file is the receipts — so the persona isn't "vibes about a TV show" but a real coaching toolkit wearing a recognizable voice.

---

## The character (voice & philosophy)

**Wendy Rhoades** — _Billions_ (Showtime, 2016–2023), created by Brian Koppelman, David Levien, and Andrew Ross Sorkin; portrayed by Maggie Siff. The character is an in-house performance coach at a hedge fund who blends psychiatric training with a trader's directness. What this project borrows: the truth-over-comfort stance, the refusal to flatter, the psychoanalytic listening (hearing what's _not_ said), and — critically — the guardrails, several of which exist precisely because the character crossed those lines on the show. The persona holds the boundaries she didn't.

The character's own consulting was informed by **Denise Shull** (real-world performance coach and author of _Market Mind Games_), who advised the show on trading psychology.

_This project is an independent, non-commercial homage. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the show or its creators._

## The techniques (real frameworks)

| Technique | Source | Framework |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Immunity Map (T5) | **Robert Kegan & Lisa Lahey**, _Immunity to Change_ (Harvard Business Review Press, 2009) | The 4-column map: commitment → competing behavior → hidden competing commitment → big assumption. This is the real diagnostic, reproduced faithfully. |
| The Complicit Question (T6) | **Jerry Colonna**, _Reboot_ (2019) / Reboot.io | "How have I been complicit in creating the conditions I say I don't want?" — Colonna's signature radical-self-inquiry question. |
| Energy Audit (T12) | **Jim Loehr & Tony Schwartz**, _The Power of Full Engagement_ (2003) | Managing energy, not time, across four dimensions: physical, emotional, mental, spiritual. |
| GROW Model (T11) | **Sir John Whitmore**, _Coaching for Performance_ (1992) | Goal → Reality → Options → Will. The most widely used coaching structure in the world. |
| Small Hit Principle (T8) | Risk-management / decision theory (deliberate bounded loss to preserve optionality) | Framing a capped, known cost as a purchase of a larger position — a discipline familiar from trading and poker. |
| Future-Self Check (T4) | Embodied / somatic decision-making; prospection research (Hal Hershfield's future-self work) | Making a decision feel real through imagined future states; the body's response as signal. |
| Wrong Voice / Tribal Identity / Earnings Confession (T1–T3) | Sports & performance psychology (confidence restoration, self-talk redirection, evidence-based self-efficacy — Bandura) | Standard pre-event state-management moves adapted to a business context. |
| Fear-Removal Question (T10) | Common coaching move; kin to Tim Ferriss's "fear-setting" and Socratic questioning | Surfacing the fear under the stated reason. |
| Pattern Interruption (T7) | Psychoanalytic / Gestalt listening | Naming the defense/deflection in the moment as data. |
| The Long Sit (T13) | Crisis-companioning & grief work (presence over problem-solving; Alan Wolfelt's "companioning" model) | Staying present without rushing to solutions; processing precedes problem-solving. |

## The ethics (professional standards)

| Guardrail area | Source |
|----------------|--------|
| Dual-relationship boundaries, scope-of-practice limits, no diagnosis | **ICF (International Coaching Federation) Code of Ethics** — the professional standard distinguishing coaching from therapy. |
| Escalate-don't-treat / crisis recognition | Standard mental-health first-aid principle: recognize, don't diagnose, refer. Crisis-line resources per region. |
| No manufactured dependency | ICF core competency: coaching fosters client autonomy; the goal is the client needing the coach less over time. |

## Additional influences

- **Marshall Goldsmith** — stakeholder-centered coaching, behavioral feedback loops (the general "coach helps high performers change specific behavior" model).
- **Bill Campbell** (_Trillion Dollar Coach_, by Eric Schmidt et al.) — "it's the people," trust as strategy; the executive-coach-to-the-valley archetype.

## A note on fidelity

Where the show and the real frameworks diverge, this project follows the **real frameworks**. The character is the voice and the stance; Kegan, Colonna, Loehr, Schwartz, Whitmore, and the ICF are the substance. That's deliberate — a coach that only imitates a TV character would be entertainment. A coach that executes real methodology in a recognizable voice is a tool.
