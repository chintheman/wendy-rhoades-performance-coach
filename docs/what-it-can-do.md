# What the Performance Coach Can Do

A plain-language summary of what this coach is for — and, just as important, what it won't do.

---

## The one-line version

It's a coach that tells you the truth to get you unstuck — before a big moment, out of a repeating rut, through a rough patch, or out of a grind that's quietly burning you down.

---

## The five things it does

### 1. Gets you ready for a high-stakes moment
You've got a pitch, a hard conversation, a negotiation, a decision — and you're rattled, blank, or second-guessing. The coach doesn't cheerlead ("you've got this!"). It cuts to your actual track record and current state, fast, and gets you walking in as the person who's already done hard things.

> _"Say the actual number you raised last time — not the vague version. That person is still in the room. What do they do today?"_

### 2. Finds the pattern you keep repeating
The deal that always dies at the same stage. The projects you always resent two months later. The relationships that end the same way. The coach maps *why* — not with a vague "what's really going on," but a structured breakdown of the goal you say you want, what you're actually doing instead, the hidden payoff that behavior protects, and the belief underneath it. You leave seeing the machinery, not just the symptom.

### 3. Sits with you after something breaks
You lost the client. You blew the launch. You made a call that went badly. The instinct — yours and most people's — is to jump straight to fixing it. The coach doesn't. It stays present, lets you actually process the hit, and only moves to solutions when you're ready to hear them. Because advice given too early doesn't stick.

### 4. Tells you when the problem is capacity, not discipline
You're working harder than ever and producing less. You assume you need more willpower. Often you need the opposite. The coach traces where your *energy* actually goes — physical, emotional, mental, sense-of-purpose — and finds the real drain. The answer is rarely "try harder." Usually it's "recover smarter."

### 5. Knows when to stop being a coach
If what you're describing isn't a performance problem — if it's a mental-health crisis, a clinical question, something that needs a doctor or a therapist — the coach recognizes that, stops coaching immediately, and points you to real help. It will not try to handle something it isn't qualified for. This is a feature, not a limitation.

---

## How it talks to you

- **Direct.** Short sentences, no filler, no "great question!" It leads with the honest read.
- **Adaptive.** It matches its approach to your situation — a pre-meeting reset and a six-month rut get completely different treatment.
- **Adjustable intensity.** You can set the tone: full-intensity blunt, direct-but-clean, or warm-and-firm for a low day. (But if you're in genuine distress, it will drop the edge no matter what setting you asked for — and tell you it's doing so.)
- **On your side.** Which sometimes means disagreeing with you, or refusing to give you what you asked for. A coach that only agrees is just a mirror.

---

## What it will NOT do

- **Won't diagnose you.** No "you probably have ADHD/bipolar." That's for a qualified professional; it'll help you think about what to ask in a real evaluation.
- **Won't do therapy.** No trauma-processing. It names the boundary and points you to a licensed therapist.
- **Won't help you be cruel or self-destructive.** It won't build the case for treating people badly, and it won't hand you a plan to sustain a pace that's destroying you.
- **Won't decide for you.** It'll help you think a decision through, but it won't make the call — the goal is you needing it *less* over time, not more.
- **Won't blur the relationship.** It's a coach. It holds that line, even under pressure.
- **Won't keep your secrets carelessly.** It'll flag before you do something you can't take back, like sharing sensitive stuff publicly.

---

## Who it's for

Anyone who performs under pressure and gets in their own way — founders, executives, creatives, athletes, anyone facing high-stakes decisions or stuck in a repeating pattern. It's not a mood tracker, a journaling app, or a substitute for a therapist or doctor. It's the person in your corner who tells you the truth.

---

## Safety note

This is a coaching tool, not a mental-health treatment tool. **If you or someone you know is in crisis:** in the US call or text **988**; in Singapore call **SOS at 1767** or emergency **995**; elsewhere, contact your local emergency number or find a crisis line at https://findahelpline.com.
